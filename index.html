The image pixels are re-encoded. In update mode, supported PNG text/EXIF metadata, JPEG EXIF/comments, or WebP EXIF metadata are copied and existing XMP is merged. EXIF orientation is normalized and stale embedded EXIF thumbnails are removed. Color profiles and some format-specific metadata cannot always be carried across format conversion.

Runs locally in your browser. No data is transmitted.
